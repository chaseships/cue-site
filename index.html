Inside

What's inside the notch.

Three small pieces do all the work: a timer you can read at a glance, a carousel that moves you forward, and a check-in that waits for you.

01

A timer line that drains.

A 3 pt line shifts from green to amber at the halfway mark to deep red at the end. It tracks a real end time, so it stays right after sleep or a restart. Pause it anytime.

02

Tasks that turn, not jump.

Finish a task and the circle fills green, becomes a check, then the next task swivels in from the right. One spring, about half a second, no bounce.

03

A check-in, not an alarm.

When time's up the island asks once: Done, or 5, 20, 30 more minutes. It never steals your keyboard, and you can dismiss it from the menu bar.

FAQ

Short answers.

Everything you might want to know before you download.

Is it free?

Yes.

Which Macs?

Macs with a notch running macOS 26 or newer.

Does it need an account or the internet?

No. Your tasks stay on your Mac.

Will it interrupt my typing?

No. Cue never takes keyboard focus; the time's-up check-in waits for a click.

Can I add more than three tasks?

No, on purpose.

Why does macOS say it can't check Cue?

Cue isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time. Open Cue once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Opening Cue the first time

  1. Open the download. Drag Cue into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Cue. macOS will say it can't check it for malware. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Cue.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. Look for the Cue icon in your menu bar.
